### [07:30 - 10:00] The High-Throughput Engineering Blueprint (200+ TPS & Sub-Second Latency)
**[SLIDE 6: High Scale, Zero Lag Architecture]**
> "Let me address the elephant in the room: **Performance, Latency, and Scalability**.
> 
> We cannot afford to have a website where pages take 3 seconds to load because an LLM is thinking. In e-commerce, every 100 milliseconds of latency drops conversion by 1 percent. And during our Big Fashion Festival, our website handles tens of thousands of requests per second and over 200 orders per second. 
> 
> Here is how our engineering team will deliver sub-second GenAI experiences:
> 
> 1. **Semantic Vector Caching**: Fashion searches have high Pareto distribution. By maintaining an in-memory vector cache with Redis and Qdrant, if two users ask for *'cocktail dress for wedding reception'*, the second user receives a cached, high-quality response in **less than 15 milliseconds**.
> 2. **Tiered Model Routing (SLM vs. LLM)**: 85% of tasks—intent classification, filter generation, and micro-copy—are handled by lightweight, quantized 2B-8B parameter models running on fast inference servers (<40ms). We only invoke heavy multimodal LLMs for complex interactive multi-turn styling.
> 3. **Server-Sent Events (SSE) & Streaming UI**: We never make users wait for a full response. We stream tokens with Time-To-First-Token (TTFT) under 200ms, progressively loading UI cards and images.
> 4. **Asynchronous Diffusion Queueing**: For Virtual Try-On, we use progressive WebGL placeholders while our distributed GPU worker pool renders the high-res texture in the background."
